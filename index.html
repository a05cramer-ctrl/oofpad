House rules.

      

        
[OOF]: One coin per game. Whoever launches first with a game's pairing line owns that game. Nobody gets it after.

        
[OOF]: The line is locked. A pump.fun description can't be edited after launch.

        
[OOF]: OOF only buys passes the game already sells. If it's not in the store, it doesn't get bought.

        
[OOF]: No passes for sale yet? The fees wait in public until there are. They never move to another game.

        
[OOF]: The dev says no → it stops for good. The queue buys back that coin and burns it.

        
[OOF]: Every purchase is made by hand on the public account, no bots. A skipped buy shows as a gap between the queue and the inventory.

        
[OOF]: OOF's cut is 0% unless it's posted here before launch.

        
[OOF]: Not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or any game on this site. No dev has agreed to anything.
